# Design Principles

## Design Direction

Editorial Craft.

Quoti should feel like a modern editorial tool rather than a traditional browser extension.

## Values

- Premium but approachable.
- Modern but timeless.
- Distinctive without being eccentric.
- Calm and enjoyable.
- Elegant but practical.

## Visual Tone

- 85% serious.
- 15% personality.

## Prefer

- Generous spacing.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.
- Soft motion.
- Subtle sculpted shadows.
- Slightly organic shapes.
- Clear, readable cards.

## Avoid

- Aggressive neobrutalism.
- AI gradient overload.
- Cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Overly technical interfaces.
- Mandatory dark mode as the main identity.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
